Meet Sergéy

A life across
creative worlds.

Interviews · Panels · Community talks
English · Belarusian · Russian

Sergéy Ulyanov is a Los Angeles-based creative and marketing strategist, musician, actor, and founder of NostalgAI Recordz, an independent label supporting musicians in exile. Originally from Belarus, he connects 7.5 years of LGBTQ+ communications and community outreach with firsthand experience of forced displacement and rebuilding a creative career in the United States.

He has used YouTube to share practical information with newcomers and help people find potential sponsors. His current work brings together music, ethical AI-assisted production, and independent research on Belarusian-language cultural memory. He speaks in English, Belarusian, and Russian about identity, belonging, creative entrepreneurship, and preserving culture across borders.
