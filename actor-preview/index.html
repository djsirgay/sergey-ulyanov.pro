04 / ABOUT

Grounded presence.
Elastic range.

“Thank you for always being cool.”Rich King Casting

Sergéy Ulyanov is a Los Angeles-based SAG-AFTRA Eligible actor with a grounded, intelligent screen presence, athletic physicality, and dry comic timing. His work spans film, television, commercials, and digital campaigns, including projects with Apple, TurboTax, HBO/Max, Samsung, Lay’s, Wargaming, and Ericsson.

He has appeared alongside Arnold Schwarzenegger, Don Cheadle, Adam DeVine, and Paul W. Downs. Trained in the Chubbuck Technique, Stanislavsky-based acting, and American accent work, Sergéy brings more than a decade of performance experience to U.S. productions.

Originally from Belarus and displaced by the war in Ukraine, he rebuilt his life and career in Los Angeles after being granted asylum in the United States. That experience informs his resilience and emotional range without replacing his identity as a working actor.
