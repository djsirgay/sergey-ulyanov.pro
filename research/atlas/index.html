03METHOD & PROVENANCE

Preservation is not permission. Discovery is not ownership.

The atlas separates public facts, archived evidence, independent research annotations, and material that requires a creator’s consent. Attribution is mandatory but does not replace permission.

  1. 01

    Find

    Locate the original source, live page, archive capture, or creator documentation.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Separate what the source proves from what remains an inference or research lead.

  3. 03

    Credit

    Name the original creators and link to their surviving pages before adding interpretation.

  4. 04

    Ask

    Request written permission before mirroring or adapting protected content.

  5. 05

    Connect

    Turn approved material into searchable context and provenance-aware research records.

Status language used across the atlas

Original workLinked directly to its creator or publisher.

Attributed recordOur annotation about a source; the source is not copied.

Independent reconstructionNew research work based on verifiable facts, clearly separated from the original.

Permissions pendingIdentified for preservation, but protected material is not publicly mirrored.
