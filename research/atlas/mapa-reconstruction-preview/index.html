MAPA made findable again.

A rights-safe prototype for exploring how a lost historical interface might be researched and rebuilt without copying its protected code, illustrations, or full dataset.

This is not the original MAPA. It is a newly designed demonstration using a small set of public historical facts. The original project remains fully credited, and no public mirror will be released without permission.

RECONSTRUCTION RULES

Preserve the idea without impersonating the original.

01

Document

Record archived captures, creators, known functionality, dates, language editions, and remaining uncertainty.

02

Rebuild independently

Use new code, new interaction design, and independently verified public facts. Do not extract or republish protected assets.

03

Ask before release

Keep the demonstration unlisted while seeking permission and expert correction. Publish only a clearly attributed, agreed scope.
