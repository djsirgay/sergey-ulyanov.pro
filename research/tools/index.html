Research tools

Start with what you want to do. Each tool has a specific job, a visible result and clear limits. You do not need to use them in order.

Music Atlas

Music search

Describe an artist, style, period or language. Explore a public metadata catalog and a separate, source-cited local collection.

You get

Source-linked works and artist leads, plus exportable search results.

Not the whole internet, a streaming player or song recognition. Missing metadata stays unknown.

Find music→ Archive Passport

Recording passport

Document a recording’s creator, context, sources and rights. Fingerprint an available file or register a missing-source recovery lead.

You get

A portable JSON passport, a citation and an event history.

A fingerprint checks exact file bytes. It does not prove authorship or identify a song across formats.

Document a recording→ Restoration Lab

Audio lab

Make a separate listening copy using conservative filters and level adjustment. Compare it with the untouched source.

You get

A WAV access copy, processing log and optional link to its source passport.

No AI reconstruction, transcription or forensic restoration. The original is never overwritten.

Compare and process audio→ Living Belarus Atlas

Culture directory

Find Belarusian-language podcasts, video, learning resources and digital heritage by topic and format.

You get

Short research notes and direct links to the original creators and publishers.

A curated starting index, not a complete archive. Linked content remains on its original website.

Explore cultural sources→ MAPA

Historical map · MAPA

See which states governed the lands of present-day Belarus. Change period, compare places and read the historical context.

You get

Dated territorial maps, country labels, place checks and source notes.

Seven mapped dates and 28 chronology stages. Context-only periods are not presented as digitized borders.
