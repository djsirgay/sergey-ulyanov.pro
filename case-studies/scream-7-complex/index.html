Paramount Pictures / Complex

SCREAM 7

A five-work poster series; “IN PROGRESS” selected for the official Complex Los Angeles exhibition.

The task

Create key art for the SCREAM 7 competition that fans would recognize before they saw the title.

My role

Concept development, visual storytelling, art direction and design for an independent competition entry.

Project gallery

Selected work

The work

What I did.

01

Turn the mask into an idea

Built five posters around familiar SCREAM motifs: the phone, surveillance, identity and the feeling of being watched. Created a five-work competition series with a common typographic treatment.

02

Choose what the viewer sees first

Made the mask emerge from a fog bank in one design. For IN PROGRESS, used a phone-list concept to turn the franchise’s recurring call into a threat before the viewer sees a face.

Result / Project status

Official competition winner; “IN PROGRESS” was selected for the Complex Los Angeles exhibition in February 2026.

Competition entry: one selected work from the original five-work series. Paramount and Complex identify the competition context.
