Franchise Campaign Concepts

Five visual directions designed to feel recognizably SCREAM while finding fresh tension between the mask, the phone, the audience, and the act of watching.

Role
Concept development · art direction · design
Output
Five-piece campaign-art series
Context
SCREAM 7 × Complex Los Angeles art exhibition, 2026
Evidence
Original artwork, exhibition material, photographs, and official event coverage
Problem

Familiar iconography can become predictable.

The work needed to preserve franchise recognition without merely repeating a Ghostface image or a conventional theatrical poster.

Process

Build one idea across five tensions.

I explored visual surveillance, audience complicity, the phone as a threat, identity behind the mask, and the clean graphic economy required for a gallery and campaign environment.

Result

A coherent series selected for exhibition.

The five works were presented in Los Angeles. Complex’s article verifies the official event context; project-specific selection evidence is shown through original materials and photographs.
