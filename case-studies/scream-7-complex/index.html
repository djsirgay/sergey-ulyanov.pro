SCREAM 7: IN PROGRESS

A five-work poster series; “IN PROGRESS” selected for the official Complex Los Angeles exhibition.

The task

Create fresh tension inside a familiar film franchise without relying on the same mask image every time.

My role

Concept development, visual storytelling, art direction and design for an independent competition entry.

The work

How it came together.

01

Explore the tension

Developed visual metaphors around the phone, surveillance, identity and the audience’s role in watching.

02

Build the series

Created a five-work competition series with reusable image and type elements.

03

Refine for impact

Reviewed legibility, franchise recognition and the strength of each idea in a gallery setting.

Result / Project status

Official competition winner; “IN PROGRESS” was selected for the Complex Los Angeles exhibition in February 2026.

Competition entry: one selected work from the original five-work series. Paramount and Complex identify the competition context.
