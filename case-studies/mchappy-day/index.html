Participation Into Measurable Impact

A charitable campaign designed as a connected participation system across website, paid social, CRM, registration, and fundraising.

Role
Digital strategy and campaign system leadership
Channels
Website · paid social · CRM · registration
Objective
Turn awareness into measurable participation and donations
Evidence
Campaign creative and reported outcomes in the current portfolio
Problem

Awareness alone does not fund a cause.

The campaign needed a low-friction path from public attention to registration, participation, and a measurable contribution.

Role & process

Connect the channel journey.

I led the digital strategy across landing experience, paid social, CRM, and registration so each touchpoint served the same participation objective.

Result

A campaign with observable action.

The portfolio reports large-scale registrations, donations, and social-audience growth. These figures are attributed to the project record rather than presented as independently audited data.
