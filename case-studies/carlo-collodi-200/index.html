Museo MAGMA / 200 per Carlo Collodi

VERIFY YOU ARE HUMAN

A Pinocchio poster that turns a familiar verification screen into a question about being human.

The task

Reinterpret Pinocchio for an international poster exhibition marking 200 years since Carlo Collodi’s birth.

My role

Poster concept, art direction, typography and final artwork.

The work

How it came together.

01

Find a contemporary test

Used the familiar “I’m human” checkbox to connect Pinocchio’s wish to become human with the way screens ask us to prove it.

02

Put the metaphor in the image

Paired a wooden hand and a lengthening nose with a smartphone, using the nose as the pointer for the verification screen.

Result / Project status

Selected for the international exhibition and catalogue.

Museo MAGMA’s selection notice named exhibitions in Italy, Argentina and Cuba and inclusion in the catalogue and archive. The international jury selected 200 posters from more than 500 submissions.
