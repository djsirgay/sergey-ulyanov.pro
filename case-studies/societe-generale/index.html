Société Générale / BelRosBank → Alfa-Bank · 2011–2014

Brand & Digital Transformation

Bank-side research and delivery across changing brands: BelRosBank (then Société Générale Group) and successor Alfa-Bank. The websites’ awards are credited to the bank named in each result, not recast as individual prizes.

Role
Competitor research · feature decisions · developer selection · bank-side delivery
Development partner
Novy Site (Новый Сайт) · external web-development company
Scope
Rebrand · digital experience · campaigns · sales modernization
Context
Project leadership on the bank side; implementation with external developers
Problem

A merger changes more than a logo.

Customers, employees, products, digital touchpoints, and market expectations all needed to align around one credible post-merger experience.

Role & process

Research the options, then direct delivery.

I led bank-side competitor and feature research, evaluated development companies, selected partners and solutions, oversaw implementation, and worked through feedback. Novy Site (Новый Сайт) handled external development; this is not a claim that I personally wrote all production code or solely authored the websites.

Result

Three documented results, with the recipient kept clear.

Novy Site’s published awards archive records TIBO-2012 third place for BelRosBank, Bank of the Year Belarus 2013 first place for Alfa-Bank’s website, and TIBO-2014 second place for Alfa-Bank. These are team-project recognitions, not individual awards.

My account of a transitional recoloring between brands is project history; a separate award for that intermediate version is not independently established. Awards for later 2018–2021 redesigns are not attributed to my work.
