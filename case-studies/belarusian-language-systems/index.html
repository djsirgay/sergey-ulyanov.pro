Language · Music · Archives · Artist infrastructure

Belarusian culture as a living system.

This is not one campaign. It is a long-running body of practice connecting Belarusian-language songwriting, video, archival curation, public storytelling, community work, and infrastructure for artists in exile.

Role
Artist · Creative producer · Curator · Founder · Public speaker
Artifacts

Research
Practice base for community-governed AI and language continuity
Boundary
Public reach is documented; national representativeness is not claimed
Problem

A language can be legally official and culturally pushed aside.

Belarusian is entangled with censorship, political identity, migration, Russian media dominance, and the forced displacement of artists. Continuity requires more than preservation: people need contemporary reasons and systems to use it.

Approach

Make culture circulate.

Sergéy used songs, visual media, performance, archives, interviews, and release infrastructure to move Belarusian language through entertainment, diaspora media, and creative commerce.

Next layer

Turn practice into auditable research.

The current research direction asks how community-governed AI can support adult language practice without flattening identity, hiding sources, or replacing human cultural authority.
