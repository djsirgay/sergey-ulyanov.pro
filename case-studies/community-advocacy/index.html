Community and advocacy

Community work

Public-health materials, youth media and first-person stories about rebuilding after displacement.

The task

Help people find useful health information, share what they have made, and tell stories in their own words.

My role

I made web and print materials, volunteered in health and youth programs, and shared my experience through interviews and community networks.

The work

What I did.

01

Public-health materials and outreach

At Vstrecha, a Global Fund partner in Belarus, I made public-health booklets, posters, web pages and annual reports. I also helped people find information on prevention and testing. This work ran from June 2007 to December 2014.

  • Read the background and press sources ↗
02

Youth media and mentoring

Supported youth media and mentoring in BELAU and UNICEF partner-initiative contexts. Volunteered as a mentor and speaker at international summer camps and created RAY, a youth magazine promoting healthy lifestyles and social awareness. The archive below documents this program work.

03

Refugee storytelling in the U.S.

I shared what it took to start over in the U.S. through interviews and practical information for newcomers. Human Rights First published my story. In September 2026 I joined the Refugee Storytellers Collective speaker pool for future opportunities; talks are agreed individually.

04

Charity events with BelRosBank

Helped organize charity activities in the BelRosBank context, including a concert for children from orphanages. The archive contains a published article about the bank’s charity activities and a group photograph from the concert.

Result / Project status

Health information in print and online, youth media work, and published stories about rebuilding a creative life.

Vstrecha / Global Fund, BELAU / UNICEF and the U.S. storytelling organizations are separate contexts. StoryRise mentorship is in progress.
