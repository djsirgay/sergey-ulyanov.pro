X-Factor / Main Stage

Rehearsals, contestant coordination, graphics and backstage video for Ksenia’s 2015 X-Factor run.

The task

Help prepare a performer for live rounds while the staging, production and backstage work moved on different schedules.

My role

I assisted producer Elena Kiper on Walter Afanasieff’s team, working across rehearsals, contestant coordination, graphics and backstage video.

The work

What I did.

01

Support rehearsals and staging

Worked with the producer and team on rehearsal preparation and coordination with the contestant.

02

Prepare graphics and backstage video

Contributed production graphics and backstage video alongside the work going into each live round.

Result / Project status

Ksenia reached the superfinal. I supported the production team during her run.

Production support through EK Publishing & Production. The competition result belongs to the performer and team.
