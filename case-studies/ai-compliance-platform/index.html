EHS Mentor

I designed and built a safety-training prototype that turns documents into assignments and lets administrators track completion.

The task

Give safety administrators a way to go from source documents and training requirements to course assignments and completion records in one place.

My role

Designed the full interface, set up servers and configured Amazon Bedrock. Built and presented a Python/FastAPI prototype.

Project gallery

Selected work

The work

What I did.

01

Design for administrators and learners

Mapped who assigns courses, who completes them, and what each person needs to see. Designed the interface for assignments, progress reporting and administrative review.

02

Set up the AI and server infrastructure

Configured Amazon Bedrock and set up servers during the program. Built the app with Python/FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker and Render.

03

Connect documents to the training workflow

Used Amazon Bedrock to extract training requirements from safety documents. Built screens to turn requirements into assignments and track completion. Screenshots show example data from the prototype.

Result / Project status

I presented a working prototype at the Cal Poly DxHub × AWS AI Summer Camp.

Prototype built during the program. I designed the interface, configured servers and Amazon Bedrock, and built the Python/FastAPI application. The public demo is a prototype, not a deployed compliance product.
