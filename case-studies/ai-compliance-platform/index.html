AI Compliance Platform

A policy-to-training workflow designed to reduce repetitive administration while keeping people responsible for review, assignment, and compliance decisions.

Role
Solo product concept, workflow design, prototype, and presentation
Context
Cal Poly DxHub × AWS AI Summer Camp, 2025
Stack
Python 3 · Amazon Bedrock · RAG · role-based dashboards
Status
Applied prototype; projected benefits are labeled as targets
Problem

Policy work was disconnected from action.

Organizations need to turn changing EHS documents into training assignments, status visibility, and auditable reporting without manually rebuilding the same chain every time.

Role & process

Map the workflow before adding AI.

I defined user roles, document ingestion, question-answer support, assignment logic, reporting states, and administrative views, then connected them in a working Python prototype using Amazon Bedrock.

Result

A usable system, not a chatbot demo.

The prototype showed an end-to-end operating model. Time and visibility figures below are project targets reported in the portfolio, not audited production outcomes.

Up to 2×targeted faster reporting workflow
