Privacy, in plain language.

This portfolio collects only what is useful to understand how people use it and to receive an inquiry. Analytics are optional.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 loads only after a visitor chooses “Allow analytics.” The site disables advertising signals and requests anonymized measurement. A visitor can decline and still use every public page. The choice is stored in the browser’s local storage.

Inquiry form

The contact form sends the name, email, organization if supplied, inquiry type, and message to Sergéy through FormSubmit. FormSubmit states that submissions may be retained for 30 days. Do not send government identifiers, banking data, immigration records, medical information, or other sensitive documents through this form. Email directly if a protected channel is needed.

External links

This site links to Google Drive, LinkedIn, YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, publishers, and project partners. Their own privacy terms apply after a visitor leaves this site.
