Global English Atlas

Research question: How can a source-grounded digital interface organize global English accent evidence for learnability and comparison without collapsing variation into stereotypes?

Principles

  • Speaker-level evidence remains visibly tied to its source and provenance.
  • One speaker does not represent a country, region, ethnicity, or social group.
  • The interface does not produce automated “accent scores.”
  • Coverage is explicitly incomplete and should be treated as a research sample, not a census of global English.
  • Uncertainty and expert-review status are first-class metadata.

Source archive

This prototype uses short factual metadata and links to the International Dialects of English Archive (IDEA). The app does not bundle or redistribute IDEA audio. Researchers open the original IDEA sample page to listen to source material. Any local audio attachment is browser-local and is not uploaded.

Research status

Private prototype / research artifact. Not a published dataset, not a validated pedagogical intervention, and not a claim of comprehensive accent coverage.

Future architecture

The current version is deliberately local-first. A future semantic-search backend can be added through a service abstraction without changing the research data model. No AWS services are connected in this version.
