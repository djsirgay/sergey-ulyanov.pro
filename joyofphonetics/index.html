Why this exists

Archives are evidence.
A workflow makes them usable.

Joy of Phonetics is not an accent generator and does not score whether a voice is “correct.” It helps actors, coaches, and researchers move from a primary-source speaker to documented listening, practice, and annotation.

IDEA

Roughly 1,800 primary-source accent and dialect samples from around the world, with biographical details, transcriptions, and commentary on many records.

Open IDEA ↗

GMU Speech Accent Archive

Thousands of speakers reading one standardized passage. Audio and derived materials are published under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Open GMU archive ↗

Method

Each speaker is an exemplar, never a proxy for a nation or social group. Observation, source commentary, inference, and coaching hypothesis should remain distinct.
