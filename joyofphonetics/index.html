About the project

Joy of Phonetics is a learning interface built around provenance.

It is designed as a research artifact for studying how authentic accent evidence can be organized for actors and language learners without flattening linguistic variation into national stereotypes.

What it is

A source-visible atlas

Records are tied to original speaker pages. The interface stores short factual metadata, annotations, and local practice data.

What it is not

Not an accent authority

No automated scoring, no “correct accent” grade, no claim that a single speaker represents a population.

Source archive

IDEA

The International Dialects of English Archive is the source archive for the indexed speaker records. Audio remains at IDEA; this site does not mirror source recordings.
