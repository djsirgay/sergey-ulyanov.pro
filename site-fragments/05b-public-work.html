Who gets to teach AI what a culture means?

Unmute Belarus is an independent project about how AI uses sources on Belarusian culture and language. Joy of Phonetics is a pronunciation practice prototype.

In a GUCHNA diagnostic, 13 of 28 test requests reached the ranking stage. The technical report explains what the earlier failures revealed about the system.
