Who gets to teach AI what a culture means?

I explore source-visible AI, cultural memory and language learning through Unmute Belarus, an archival research practice, and Joy of Phonetics, a formative pronunciation-learning prototype.

The work connects original sources, interpretation and review. Methods and project status are public; learning outcomes are still questions to test.
