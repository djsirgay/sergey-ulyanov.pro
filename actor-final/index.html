Sergéy Ulyanov is a Belarusian-born, Los Angeles-based SAG-AFTRA Eligible actor working across film, television, commercials, digital media, and voiceover. His casting lane includes intelligent professionals, authority figures, athletic characters, Eastern European roles, and dry or offbeat comedy.

After leaving Ukraine during the war, Sergéy rebuilt his life and career in Los Angeles — bringing more than a decade of performance experience, multilingual range, and lived resilience to the screen.
