Sergéy Ulyanov is a Los Angeles-based SAG-AFTRA Eligible actor working across film, television, commercials, digital media, and voiceover. His screen presence supports authority figures, intelligent professionals, athletic characters, Eastern European roles, and dry or offbeat comedy.

He brings more than a decade of performance experience, ongoing Los Angeles training in the Chubbuck Technique and American accent work, Stanislavsky-based training, and a degree in directing and theatrical production.
