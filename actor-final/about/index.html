Sergéy Ulyanov is a Belarusian-born, Los Angeles-based commercial actor whose work spans national and international campaigns, branded content, digital media, film, and voiceover.

His strongest casting lanes combine intelligence, grounded authority, physical confidence, and dry or offbeat comedy. A trained General American accent complements his native Eastern European and Russian sound.

After leaving Ukraine during the war, he rebuilt his life and career in Los Angeles. That experience informs a calm, prepared, collaborative presence on set—without becoming the story he is there to serve.
